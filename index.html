    How it plays
    • ShardsEach shard points at its key. Press ↑ → ↓ ← or WASD as it crosses the ring.
    • RingsA ring closing on the core is a bass hit. Press Space as it lands.
    • ClarityMisses muffle the song. If it fades out completely, the run ends.
    • FuseBefore a drop the ring burns down. Hit the gold ring on the drop for double points.
